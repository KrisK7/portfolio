## My Past

My journey in digital design began within the traditional agency model. While I learned invaluable lessons about branding and process, I grew frustrated with the inefficiency: the endless layers of approval, the miscommunication between departments, and the significant portion of a client's budget spent on overhead and internal meetings—not on the actual design thinking that moves the needle.

A fundamental disagreement on this very principle—that client value should be centered on outputs and outcomes, not on billed hours and hierarchy—led me to strike out on my own.

But I didn't just become another freelancer. I built a new kind of practice. I am a strategic UX/UI partner augmented by local Large Language Models. This partnership allows me to operate with the rigor and systematic thinking of a top-tier agency, but with the focus, speed, and direct accountability of a solo expert.

## Local-First AI

Sensitive client work is processed on secure local LLMs. Cient data never leaves my system, ensuring privacy and reducing cloud energy use.

My LLM co-pilot handles the busywork: synthesizing research data, generating user persona variations, drafting copy frameworks, and keeping documentation flawless. This frees me to focus on what truly matters: the high-level strategy, the nuanced user psychology, the elegant interface solutions, and the deep collaboration with clients.
